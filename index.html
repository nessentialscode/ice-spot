Know Us

The Central Ice Cream Production Factory in Varavoor

Situated in Varavoor, Wadakkanchery, Thrissur, Ice Spot Varavoor is our primary manufacturing factory and central franchise headquarters, dedicated to crafting delicious handcrafted ice creams, refreshing shakes, and artisanal ice candies.

As the heart of Ice Spot's production, our Varavoor factory crafts our signature 10 real fruit sticks and supplies parlours across Kerala with authentic recipes, rich cream, and pure, sun-ripened ingredients.
